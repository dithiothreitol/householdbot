# Gotchas — subscriptions-review

- **Yearly plans hide in one month.** A yearly charge appears once; with only
  three months of export it is invisible. Ask for a longer export or check the
  register.
- **The card statement names the processor.** "APPLE.COM/BILL" or "GOOGLE
  *" can cover several services; ask which.
- **Two occurrences are a guess.** Three make a pattern; say which it is.
- **A price rise after a promotion is expected, but still a decision.** Show
  it with the date it started.
- **Shared accounts.** A family plan paid by one member covers others; do not
  call it a duplicate without asking.
