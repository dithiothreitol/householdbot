# Gotchas — life-event

- **The day it happened, not today.** Every window counts from the event:
  the purchase date on the contract, the date of birth, the date of death.
  Ask for it; never assume today.
- **Dealer or person matters.** A car bought from a VAT dealer and one bought
  from a neighbour have different tax steps; ask before listing them.
- **Some steps are already done.** Hospitals, dealers and funeral homes often
  handle one step for the family; ask and drop it, do not remind about it.
- **Planned events move.** A move planned for the 30th counts from the 30th;
  when the date changes, recount, do not shift by hand.
- **No checklist, no invented law.** For an event the pack does not cover,
  the household's own list carries no legal dates.
- **Loss needs a slow pace.** Two or three next steps, then stop and offer
  to continue; never a wall of deadlines on the first day.
- **Sympathy comes first.** In testing an answer opened with a remark about
  the language it was written in and offered no sympathy at all; the first
  sentence is the sympathy sentence.
- **The profile changes too.** A new child, a sold car, a new address: hand
  the change to household-customize, or the other skills keep the old facts.
- **What ends with the event.** A sold car still has its OC renewal and
  roadworthiness test in the register; name them in the first answer and
  offer to close them after a yes.
