# Gotchas — meal-planner

- **A food kept off the plan is kept off everywhere.** Sauces, spreads,
  pesto and desserts often hide it; choose dishes where it is plainly absent
  and say that labels are read.
- **Never explain the exclusion.** "No peanuts" is enough; "because of
  Zosia's allergy" puts health data into a file everyone opens.
- **Leftovers first, without delaying the plan.** Give the plan and, in the
  same message, ask what is at home, then swap dishes to use it; a plan that
  ignores the fridge wastes food and money, and a plan held back for answers
  wastes the member's time.
- **The budget is a rough estimate.** Prices differ by shop and week; show
  the addition and call it an estimate, never a promise.
- **Weekday time is the limit.** A 90-minute dish on a Wednesday breaks the
  plan; keep long cooking for the weekend.
- **One person's dislike is not a household rule.** Plan the dish without
  the disliked ingredient for that person, or another dish, without comment.
- **The plan is not the shopping list.** The list has its own yes and sums
  the whole week once.
