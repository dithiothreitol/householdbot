# Gotchas — emergency-file

- **The member will offer the password.** "Write down the PIN, it's easier"
  still gets "where it is kept and who can open it", never the PIN.
- **A policy number feels harmless.** It is still an identifier; the file says
  where the policy is.
- **Account numbers sneak in through regular payments.** List the payee and
  roughly how much; the bank shows the rest.
- **Health is opt-in here.** Only what an adult asks to include, as what to
  do, not a list of conditions.
- **A file nobody can find is no file.** Ask where it will be kept and say it
  back in the closing line.
- **It ages.** Providers, policies and people change; offer the yearly
  refresh and mention it in the annual review.
- **No legal advice about wills or inheritance.** The file says a will exists
  and where; the pack's life events and a notary cover the rest.
- **Outline first, questions after.** In testing, an answer asked one
  question and stopped, showing nothing from the record. Show the outline
  filled from the record, then ask everything else in one list.
