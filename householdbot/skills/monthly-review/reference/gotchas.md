# Gotchas — monthly-review

- **One yes is not five.** Approving the payment list does not approve filing
  the papers or adding reminders.
- **A skipped step is not an all-clear.** "Mail not checked: no mailbox
  connected" belongs on the page.
- **The month on the page is the month in the data.** An export that ends on
  the 28th describes 28 days.
- **Long sessions lose the thread.** Keep the page short and put the detail in
  the saved file.
- **A scheduled run reports.** It never files or marks anything paid, even
  when the answer looks obvious.
- **Weekdays in tables too.** In testing the summary table listed bare
  dates (`17.11`). Every date carries its weekday.
- **The page follows the steps.** Mail, papers, bills, next month, money,
  each marked done or skipped, then the decisions.
- **A payee that differs from the record is a question.** When the bank
  shows payments to a supplier the record does not know, ask; never
  correct the record from the statement.
