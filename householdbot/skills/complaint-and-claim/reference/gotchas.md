# Gotchas — complaint-and-claim

- **A guarantee is extra, not instead.** A guarantee card does not take away
  the trader's liability for non-conformity; choose the route that serves the
  member.
- **The clock starts at delivery**, not at the order and not at the payment.
- **Silence can be an answer.** Under Polish law a trader who does not reply
  to a consumer complaint within 14 days accepts it; banks and insurers have
  their own period. Track the date.
- **Withdrawal needs no reason**, but it has a short window; sending the
  statement before it ends is enough.
- **Photos before the repair.** Evidence is gathered before the item goes
  back or gets fixed.
- **The shop's form is a channel, not a requirement.** A complaint is valid
  in any form the law allows; keep proof of when it was sent.
- **Draft first, ask alongside.** In testing the answer held the complaint
  back until the shop's name and dates were known. When the route is
  clear, give the draft with placeholders and the questions under it.
- **The pack's term.** A consumer's purchase from a trader is *niezgodność
  towaru z umową*; *rękojmia* belongs to other contracts.
- **Read the pack before answering from memory.** In testing, with no
  shell, an answer guessed that silence from an insurer means nothing. The
  pack (`financial-complaint-reply`) says the complaint is then accepted,
  and `silence` in the window says so for each kind of complaint.
