# Gotchas — home-upkeep

- **A seasonal job is not a duty.** Gutters, the boiler service and outside
  taps are the household's own reminders; only the checks the country pack
  lists carry a law, and only that law is named.
- **The guarantee hides in an invoice.** Guarantee terms are often printed on
  an invoice or a receipt filed as `receipt`, not on a separate card; search
  by words, not by document type.
- **Guarantee and trader's liability are two clocks.** The card's guarantee
  and the trader's statutory liability run separately and can end on
  different days; complaint-and-claim chooses the route for a fault.
- **The next check counts from the last one.** Without the date of the last
  inspection the next one is unknown, never "a year from today"; and
  `record.py complete` counts from the old due date, so a check done early
  needs its next date counted from the day it was done.
- **A flat's checks may not be the household's to order.** In a shared
  building, ask who orders them before registering anything; the household's
  part may only be access on the day.
- **The cheaper quote often leaves something out.** Materials, removing
  rubble or VAT: put the same scope side by side before comparing totals.
- **A cash advance leaves no trace.** Ask for a receipt, or a transfer to the
  account on the contract; the payment itself is the member's to arrange.
- **"Not in the record" is not "never done".** A repair without a filed
  invoice still happened; ask before calling the history complete.
- **"Not stated" is not "none".** In testing, a comparison turned a quote's
  silence into "no warranty" and "without a receipt". The table and the flags
  say "not stated" and ask.
- **No file to prepare an entry.** An entry goes to `record.py` on standard
  input; in testing, a file written to prepare one counted as a write before
  the yes.
