# Gotchas — insurance-overview

- **The car policy is filed under the car.** Compulsory vehicle insurance
  often sits in category `vehicle`, not `insurance`; collect policies by
  document type and by words, not by one category.
- **Private liability comes bundled.** It sits inside home policies, bank and
  card packages and travel cover; ask what each covers before calling two of
  them a duplicate.
- **Two covers may each pay a part.** Overlapping policies can share a claim
  rather than pay it twice; describe the overlap, do not conclude.
- **A policy that does not renew just ends.** Its reminder is the end date,
  with time to compare offers; there is nothing to cancel.
- **No last year, no rise.** Without an earlier policy in the record the
  premium cannot be compared; say so instead of calling it unchanged.
- **A gap is a question, not a sale.** "The record has no policy for X" asks
  the member to file one or say there is none; it never suggests buying.
- **School cover may never reach the folder.** Accident cover bought through
  the school often comes without a policy at home; ask before calling a child
  uncovered.
- **A package pays the insurer for you.** Cover included in a bank or card
  package is paid inside its fee, so no insurer appears on the statement;
  ask about packages before comparing payees with the table.
