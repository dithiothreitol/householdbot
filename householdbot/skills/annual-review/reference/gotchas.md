# Gotchas — annual-review

- **"May go" is not "gone".** The plugin lists; the household shreds and
  deletes; the entry is marked `disposed` only after the member says it is
  done.
- **A keeping date needs a basis.** Tax papers follow the limitation period,
  receipts the complaint period; where no statute sets one, the pack marks it
  "suggested" and so does the answer.
- **Identity, property and court papers stay.** They are kept for good unless
  the household decides otherwise for a superseded one.
- **Archive is not delete.** Closed reminders move to `archive/` and can be
  read there; nothing is lost.
- **The pack ages.** A pack reviewed more than a year ago may carry old
  amounts and deadlines; say so before anyone relies on it.
- **One sitting, many approvals.** A yes to archiving is not a yes to setting
  keeping dates; ask each time.
- **The emergency file is part of the year.** People, policies and providers
  change; refresh it here.
