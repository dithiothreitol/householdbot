# Gotchas — shopping-list

- **Two lines for one product.** "Masło" from the meal plan and "masło 2
  szt." from Piotr are one line: "masło 3 szt." Show the sum.
- **Units that do not add up.** 1 kg and 500 g of flour sum to 1.5 kg; a
  packet and grams do not, so keep both on one line.
- **A dictated item needs no question.** Asking "should I add milk?" after
  "add milk" is friction; add it and say so.
- **A proposed item does.** Ingredients from the meal plan and staples are
  shown first, because the household may have them at home.
- **Ticked is not deleted.** Clear bought items only after a yes; someone may
  still be in the shop.
- **Shop order, not alphabet.** Group by the household's shops and the order
  of the trip, so the list reads like the route.
- **No basket, no order.** Even when a shop's app is mentioned, the list stays
  a file the member uses.
