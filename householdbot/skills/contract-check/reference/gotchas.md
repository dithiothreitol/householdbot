# Gotchas — contract-check

- **The price is in the price list, not the contract.** Many contracts point
  to a separate price list or terms; read them or say they are missing.
- **"Indefinite" after the fixed term** usually means a higher price and a
  short notice period; say both.
- **The early-termination fee has a formula.** Quote it and work one example
  ("leaving after 13 months costs …") only from the document's own numbers.
- **Consents are not all required.** Marketing consents are optional; say
  which ones the contract needs.
- **Signed online or at the door changes the withdrawal right.** The country
  pack gives the window; signing in a shop usually gives none.
