# Gotchas — cancel-or-switch

- **Sent is not received.** For a contract, notice counts when it reaches the
  provider. A letter posted on the last day is late.
- **"One month" is a calendar month.** Thirty days and one month end on
  different days; use the contract's wording with `--unit months`.
- **The fixed term ends, the contract does not.** Many contracts continue
  indefinitely after the fixed term at a higher price; the notice date is
  before the end of the fixed term.
- **Compulsory insurance has no gaps.** End the old vehicle policy only with
  the new one in place; the country pack names the rules.
- **The confirmation is the proof.** Track it. Without it the household finds
  out at the next invoice.
- **Email may not be enough.** When the contract requires a signed letter,
  say so before the member relies on an email.
- **A follow-up check is not a deadline.** The "confirmation received"
  reminder 14 days after sending is the household's own check; say so,
  so nobody reads it as a legal window.
