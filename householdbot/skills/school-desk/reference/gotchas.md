# Gotchas — school-desk

- **"By Friday" needs a date.** A relative date in a message counts from the
  day the message was written, not the day it was read; write the date with
  its weekday.
- **The payee is often missing.** A letter that asks for money without saying
  to whom gets a question for the teacher, never a guessed account.
- **Cash goes on a school day.** Money sent with the child must leave on a
  school day before the deadline; a day off just before it moves the plan
  earlier.
- **One trip, three dates.** A trip usually brings a consent, a payment and
  the day itself; keep them as separate lines with separate dates.
- **The same item, three times.** The e-register, an email copy and a paper
  note often carry the same request; match against the register before
  adding.
- **A consent in the e-register is the member's.** No connector reaches
  Polish e-registers; never say a consent was given there, and keep the
  reminder open until the member says it is done.
- **A health form is not a calendar item.** A medical check or a diet form is
  listed as "form to return"; what it says stays with the adults.
- **A weekday and a date that disagree.** "Friday 5 December" in a year when
  the 5th is a Saturday is reported and asked about, never resolved by
  picking one.
- **The register entry is a summary.** In testing, an answer built from the
  entry's key facts said the room and the place were not given; the filed
  letter named both. Open the letter itself before calling anything "not
  stated".
