# Gotchas — inbox-triage

- **A label search by ID returns nothing.** Search by the label's display
  name, and cross-check an empty result with the label list.
- **Real office mail rarely arrives as a plain email.** In Poland official
  letters come by registered post, e-Doręczenia or ePUAP; an email "from the
  tax office" with a payment link is a scam pattern.
- **One-time codes are not information.** Count the sign-in mail; never quote
  the code.
- **A delivery update is not a decision.** A parcel surcharge with a link is
  suspicious, not "needs a decision".
- **The PDF is the bill, the email is the envelope.** Amount and due date come
  from the attachment when there is one; the email text may round or omit them.
- **Seven days is a window, not the mailbox.** Say which period was searched.
