# Gotchas — weekly-brief

- **"Nothing due" from an empty register is not good news.** A brief with no
  items says how many obligations the register holds and what was not
  connected.
- **Overdue first, even when it is small.** A forgotten PLN 40 water bill
  above a PLN 1,200 renewal: the overdue item leads.
- **The one thing is one.** Pick by consequence (a closing legal window, a
  contract that renews at a higher price, a fee for lateness), then by date.
- **Health appointments are times, not reasons.** "Appointment, Thu 10:30".
- **Calendar times come from the offset,** printed in the household's zone.
  A work calendar is not read (storage scope).
- **A brief saved is not a brief scheduled.** Only claim a schedule that
  exists.
