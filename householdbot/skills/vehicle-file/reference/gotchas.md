# Gotchas — vehicle-file

- **The renewal day is the wrong reminder.** For a policy that renews by
  itself, the date that matters is the last day to give notice, from the
  policy and the pack's rule; by the renewal day it is too late.
- **A test date from memory.** The remembered date is often a year off; ask
  the member to read it from the papers and record it as
  `told by household member`.
- **Kilometres have no date.** A service due at a reading cannot be put on a
  day; keep the reading in the notes and use the months limit as the date.
- **A ticket's clock starts at acceptance.** The window runs from the day the
  ticket was accepted, not from the day the member mentions it.
- **A camera letter is not a ticket.** A letter asking the owner to name the
  driver has its own deadline from delivery; it goes to reply-to-letter.
- **Fake fines arrive by text message.** A link to pay a fine, a toll or a
  parking penalty is checked against the pack's scam patterns and never
  opened.
- **The car is its label, not its numbers.** The VIN, the registration
  certificate and the policy number stay in the papers; the register says
  which page.
- **A sold car keeps its reminders.** Close its renewal, test and service
  entries after the sale, or the household is reminded about a car it no
  longer owns.
