# Gotchas — household-customize

- **A sold car is two changes.** The profile row and the open obligations
  that name the car. Showing only the first leaves a renewal reminder for a
  car the household no longer owns.
- **A sale often creates duties.** In many countries the seller must notify
  the insurer or the registration office within a set number of days. Offer
  them from the country pack as new obligations, tagged
  `country pack rule — verify`.
- **Changing the language changes answers, not the past.** From the yes on,
  answers, briefs, dates, amounts and new log lines follow the new language.
  Register field names and statuses stay in English; earlier titles, notes and
  log lines stay as they were written.
- **The profile's headings are a separate question.** Switching `Language`
  does not require rewriting the headings; offer it as its own yes.
- **The threshold applies forward.** Raising the two-adult threshold does not
  re-approve anything approved before.
- **"Remove Piotr" for a member who left** keeps his past entries as they
  are; only open obligations need a new owner.
