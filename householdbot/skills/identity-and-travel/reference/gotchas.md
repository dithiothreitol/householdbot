# Gotchas — identity-and-travel

- **Valid on the way out, expired on the way back.** Check the expiry
  against the day of return, not the day of leaving.
- **Some countries count beyond the stay.** How long a document must stay
  valid after the trip differs by destination and changes; read the official
  page for that country every time.
- **A child's document runs shorter.** Children's documents are issued for a
  shorter time than adults'; check the child's own expiry, never assume it
  matches a parent's.
- **"No passport in the record" is not "no passport".** Ask before telling a
  member to apply for one.
- **A reminder needs no number.** The register holds the expiry and where the
  document is kept; a booking form takes the number from the document itself.
- **The EKUZ card is not travel insurance.** It covers what the pack and the
  official page say, where they say; more cover is a question for
  insurance-overview, not advice.
- **An identity card may not be enough.** Whether a destination accepts it is
  on the official page, not in memory.
- **The lead time is a suggestion, not a promise.** An office can take longer
  in the weeks before the holidays; start when the reminder says, not at the
  last moment.
